Why AI

The work is inherited. The responsibility is ours.

Contributing to AI feels like a chance to help carry humanity’s work forward.

None of us begins from zero. We inherit medicine, science, infrastructure, and ideas from people who spent their lives pushing the boundary a little further. Their work quietly shapes what the rest of us are able to do.

I see AI as the next major step in that chain. We do not know exactly where it leads, but being born while it is still taking shape feels like a privilege—and contributing to it is one way to pay that inheritance forward.
