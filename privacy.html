Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Daniel Mora (“we”, “me”, or “I”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use daniel-mora.com and related applications or integrations that rely on Meta (Facebook) Platform APIs or TikTok developer APIs.

Who this applies to

This policy applies to visitors of this website and, where stated, to users of software or integrations I operate that connect to Meta or TikTok services.

Information we may collect

  • Website usage. Standard server or hosting logs may include IP address, approximate location, browser type, referring URLs, and timestamps.
  • Site analytics. A cookieless script records which pages you view, which sections you scroll to, which links and buttons you click, how long you stay, and the referring page (its address without any query string), along with your approximate city and country (derived from your IP address by the host), browser, operating system, screen size, language, and time zone. A random visit ID lives only in the open page and is never stored on your device. To group the pages you view on one day, the server also derives a short visitor code from your IP address and browser combined with a random value that is replaced daily and deleted after two days; your IP address itself is not stored, and the code cannot be linked across days. Aggregate counts and the most recent 100 visits are kept; the step-by-step record of each visit (pages, sections, clicks, how far you scrolled, questions asked to the chat assistant and time on page) is deleted after 30 days.
  • Chat assistant (MORA-01). If you use the on-site chat, your messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate replies. The text of your questions and the assistant's replies, with your approximate country and city, is logged (the most recent 200) to improve the site. Please do not enter personal or sensitive information in the chat.
  • Communications. If you contact me by email or submit the TC_ACC case-request form, I process the contents of your message and your contact details to respond.
  • Data from Meta or TikTok. When you authorize an app that uses Meta or TikTok OAuth, Meta or TikTok may share identifiers and permitted profile or content-related data according to your permissions and their policies. I only request data needed for stated app features.

How we use information

  • To operate and improve this website and any linked applications.
  • To authenticate you and deliver features you have authorized via Meta or TikTok.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect security.

Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where GDPR applies, processing is based on: your consent where required (for example OAuth approvals); contractual necessity where you use my services; and legitimate interests in operating secure, reliable websites and integrations, balanced against your rights.

Sharing and processors

I do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliated companies, when your use involves Meta APIs—subject to Meta’s Terms and Policies.
  • TikTok and its service providers when your use involves TikTok APIs—subject to TikTok’s developer and user policies.
  • Infrastructure providers (for example hosting on Vercel or similar), solely to operate the site and services.
  • Google (Gemini API), solely to generate chat assistant replies.
  • Upstash, the database that stores analytics counts, recent visits, and recent chat questions and replies.
  • Email delivery providers (currently Resend), solely to deliver messages submitted through the TC_ACC request form.

Retention

I retain data only as long as needed for the purposes above, unless a longer period is required by law. OAuth tokens or API-related data are refreshed or deleted when no longer necessary for the product.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For Meta-specific data handling, Meta also provides privacy settings and dashboards in accordance with Meta’s policies. For TikTok, see TikTok’s in-app privacy tools and policies.

To exercise GDPR-related requests regarding data I control, contact me at the email below.

Data deletion instructions

To disconnect an integration that uses Meta or TikTok, open your Meta (Facebook/Instagram) or TikTok account settings and remove app access according to each platform’s instructions. To request deletion of personal data processed by Daniel Mora in connection with a specific app or integration, email damorasoler@gmail.com with the subject line “Data deletion request” and the name of the app or product.

Cookies

This site is lightweight and may use only essential cookies or similar technologies required for hosting or security. Third-party integrations you load in your browser may set their own cookies according to their policies.

This site does not set analytics cookies or place identifiers on your device. The usage statistics described above are not shared with advertisers.

Children

My services are not directed at children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

International transfers

Data may be processed in Spain, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or other regions where processors operate. Appropriate safeguards apply where required by law.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
