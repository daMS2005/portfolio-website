Sixteen years, as the encoder saw it

2010Jan

Every month-end, about 1,000 US companies, as one self-supervised encoder sees them.Its strongest direction is risk: calm companies left, volatile ones right. Bright: this month. Grey: every month before. In colour: Walmart, GameStop, Carnival, Intel.

October 2011: the whole market slides toward volatile.No month-end sits further right until 2020.

Then calmer years. Walmart barely leaves the calm edge.From 2012 to 2017 its month-end dots stay almost entirely on the far left.

Early 2018: GameStop moves to the volatile edge, three years before the squeeze.It stays there into 2025.

February 2020: Carnival crosses the map. In March, nearly every company follows.March and April 2020 are the most volatile month-ends in sixteen years. Carnival stays on the volatile side into 2024.

2022: a slower slide that lasts the whole year.Every month-end of 2022 sits right of the sixteen-year average.

Intel, a calm large cap in 2014, now sits with the volatile.The map is a UMAP of the encoder's embeddings, rotated so risk runs left to right.
